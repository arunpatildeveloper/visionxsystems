Research Completed

01

FaceForensics++

Rössler et al., 2019

  • 1,000 real YouTube videos, ~1.8M frames
  • Faked with 4 methods: 2 face swaps (DeepFakes, FaceSwap) and 2 expression edits (Face2Face, NeuralTextures)
  • Tested at 3 quality levels: raw, light and heavy compression
  • Best detector (XceptionNet): 99.26% raw · 95.73% light compression · 81% heavy compression
  • Humans scored below 50% on the subtlest fakes
arxiv.org/abs/1901.08971
02

DFDC — Deepfake Detection Challenge

Dolhansky et al., 2020  ·  Meta

  • 100,000+ clips from 3,426 paid, consenting actors
  • Varied ages, skin tones, lighting and backgrounds; multiple fake methods
  • Global Kaggle challenge with a public test set and a hidden unseen test set
  • Top models dropped clearly on the hidden unseen videos
arxiv.org/abs/2006.07397
